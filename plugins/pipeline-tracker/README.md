# Pipeline Tracker

Track every pursuit from first whisper to won or lost, and what each cost.

Keeps one dated record of every pursuit the practice is chasing, from the first mention of a project — a call, a rumor, a client hint — through qualification, the go decision, the proposal, and the outcome, with the effort each pursuit consumed and the reason each loss happened. Leads every read-back with the pursuits consuming effort that have no go decision on record, because chasing without deciding is where business development money quietly goes. Trigger on "pipeline tracker", "what are we chasing", "track our pursuits", "proposals out right now", "did we ever hear back on that RFP", "what is our win rate", "pipeline-tracker", or a pile of pursuit emails handed over to make sense of. Carries no win-rate benchmarks, no hit-rate targets, and no probability weightings; the practice's history is the only pattern it reports.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
