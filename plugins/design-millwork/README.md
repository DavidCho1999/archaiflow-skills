# Design Millwork

Detail built-in joinery so the shop can price it and the wall can hold it.

Details built-in joinery to the point a shop can price it without asking: what is fixed and what moves, how it meets a floor and a wall that are neither level nor straight, what it hangs off and whether that backing exists, where the services in it come from, and which decisions are the shop's rather than the drawing's. Trigger on "millwork", "casework", "joinery detail", "built-in", "reception desk", "how does this cabinet meet the wall", "design-millwork", or a built-in element that has to be drawn for tender. Carries no dimensions, no clearances, no material thicknesses and no hardware; ergonomic and accessible dimensions come from the standard you quote and every material figure from the fabricator or the data sheet. It is not shop drawing — `submittal-review` reads those when they come back — and does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
