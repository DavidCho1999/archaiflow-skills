# Area Measurement

Establish which area standard governs and what actually counts in it.

Settles the question every project answers three times and differently: which area standard is being used, where its boundary is drawn, and what counts toward it. A building has a zoning floor area, a code floor area, a gross and a net for programming, and a leasable area, and each is measured by a different rule from a different document. Records one basis per purpose with its source, reconciles the figures that result, and reports the difference rather than resolving it into one number. Trigger on "which area standard", "gross versus net", "what counts as floor area here", "our areas do not match the lease", "BOMA measurement", "area-measurement", or two area figures that disagree. Carries no standards, no definitions and no factors; each comes from the document you quote. It measures nothing itself and does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
