# Explain Easement

Explain what an easement on your survey does to what you can build.

Explains an easement, right of way, or other title encumbrance shown on a survey in plain English, and answers the question a designer actually has: what can and cannot go there, who may enter, and what happens when it has to be crossed or moved. Distinguishes the kinds that behave differently — utility, access, drainage, conservation, party wall, air rights, prescriptive — and separates what the survey shows from what the recorded document says, because the survey is a drawing of the document and not the document. Carries no legal positions and quotes no instrument it was not given. It is not legal advice; easements are read by a title attorney and moved or released by agreement or by a court.

This is a command. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.

## Install

In the Claude app: Customize > Plugins > Add marketplace, paste `DavidCho1999/archaiflow-skills`, then install **explain-easement**.

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin install explain-easement --marketplace DavidCho1999/archaiflow-skills
```
