# Leak Triage

Find out which water this is before anyone opens anything up.

Works out what kind of water is appearing before anyone opens a wall: rain driven in, groundwater, a plumbing failure, condensation, or air-carried moisture, which look identical inside and have entirely different causes, owners and repairs. Reads the evidence that separates them — when it appears, what the weather was doing, whether it is warm or cold, whether it is clean, whether it stops — then narrows the source and names what would confirm it. Trigger on "there is a leak", "water is coming in", "a stain appeared on the ceiling", "is this condensation or a leak", "where is this water coming from", "leak-triage", or water appearing anywhere with no agreed cause. Carries no dew points, no rainfall figures and no test standards; every value comes from the building's own data or the tester. It diagnoses rather than repairs, does not apportion liability, and does not replace the enclosure consultant, the plumber, or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
