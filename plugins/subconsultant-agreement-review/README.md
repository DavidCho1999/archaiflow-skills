# Subconsultant Agreement Review

Check that your subconsultant agreements match what you promised the owner.

Reads subconsultant agreements against the prime agreement to find the gap the architect is standing in: obligations promised to the owner that were never passed down, insurance required upstream and not required downstream, payment terms that oblige payment before you are paid, and scope that appears in neither agreement. Works clause by clause across both documents and reports every mismatch with what each side says. Trigger on "review the subconsultant agreement", "does our consultant agreement match the prime", "flow down check", "who carries this obligation", "subconsultant-agreement-review", or a consultant agreement handed over before execution. Carries no standard-form text and no legal positions; both agreements come from you. It is not legal advice and does not replace the attorney or the Architect of Record. Read `contract-review` on the prime first.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
