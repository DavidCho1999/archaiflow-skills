# Counsel Engagement Triggers

This skill is procedural. When any of the triggers below fire, the memo must state explicitly: **"Engage construction counsel before next step."**

The thresholds are heuristic rules of thumb — not bright-line legal rules. The defining principle: engage counsel when the dispute is large enough that procedural error becomes more expensive than counsel's fee.

---

## 1. Claim amount thresholds

| Threshold | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| < $25K | Often resolves in field discussion / RFI; counsel optional. |
| $25K – $100K | Counsel review of strategy recommended; counsel may not need to be lead negotiator. |
| > $100K | **Engage counsel.** Federal CDA Claims > $100K require certification (41 USC §7103(b)) — counsel-only execution. |
| > $1M | **Engage counsel.** AAA Optional Procedures for Large, Complex Construction Disputes trigger ($1M default). Specialized construction counsel essential. |

In small markets or for small firms, lower the thresholds proportionally — what's a $25K dispute for a regional GC is a project-killing dispute for a small architect.

---

## 2. Termination notice (for default OR for convenience)

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Termination for default issued by Owner against Contractor | **Engage counsel immediately.** A201 §14.2 — Owner termination for cause is high-stakes; wrongful default termination becomes wrongful breach with massive damages exposure. |
| Termination for default issued by Contractor against Owner | **Engage counsel immediately.** A201 §14.1 — Contractor termination for cause has different mechanics. |
| Termination for convenience issued by Owner | **Engage counsel.** A201 §14.4 — convenience terminations have known mechanics but quantum of damages (cancellation costs, demobilization, anticipated profit on uncompleted work) is contested. |
| Notice of intent to terminate (warning notice) | **Engage counsel before issuing or responding.** Notice triggers cure-period clocks. |

---

## 3. Personal injury or property damage

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Worker injury on site (any severity) | **Engage counsel** + insurance broker / WC carrier. |
| Property damage to existing structure or third-party property | **Engage counsel** + insurance broker / GL carrier. |
| Fatality | **Engage counsel immediately** + insurance broker + OSHA reporting (8 hours) + state Workers' Comp Board reporting. |

This skill does not handle injury / property claims procedurally — these go directly to counsel + insurance.

---

## 4. Bond or insurance claim implications

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Performance bond claim contemplated | **Engage counsel** + surety. A312 procedure has specific notice / cure requirements; surety has its own counsel and deadlines. |
| Payment bond claim by sub or supplier | **Engage counsel** + surety. A312 §13 + Miller Act (federal) or state Little Miller Act. |
| Builder's Risk loss | **Engage broker** + insurance counsel. Coverage trigger and adjustment is broker / counsel. |
| PL claim against architect / EOR | **Engage PL carrier counsel.** PL policies typically require carrier consent before significant claim activity; voiding coverage is the worst possible outcome. |
| GL / CGL claim against contractor | **Engage GL carrier counsel.** Same logic as PL. |
| Cyber claim | **Engage cyber carrier counsel** + IT counsel. |

---

## 5. Federal Contract Disputes Act (CDA) on the table

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Federal-funded project + claim > $100K | **Engage counsel.** Certification required under 41 USC §7103(b). |
| Contracting Officer Final Decision (COFD) issued | **Engage counsel within days.** 90-day appeal window to ASBCA / CBCA; 12-month window to US Court of Federal Claims. |
| Federal protest contemplated | **Engage counsel.** Bid protests are time-bound (10 days to GAO; 30 days to Court of Federal Claims). |
| Termination for default on federal project | **Engage counsel immediately.** Federal default termination is harder to reverse than commercial. |

---

## 6. Mechanic's lien / stop notice / bond claim deadlines approaching

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| State lien notice deadline within 30 days | **Engage counsel.** State-specific lien procedure; missed deadline = lien rights lost. |
| Stop-notice deadline (CA, OR, others) within 30 days | **Engage counsel.** |
| Miller Act Payment Bond suit must be filed within 1 year of last furnishing | **Engage counsel** with at least 90 days to spare. |
| State Little Miller Act deadline | **Engage counsel.** State-specific. |

This skill does not advise on lien / bond mechanics — refer to counsel.

---

## 7. Multiple-party disputes / consolidation issues

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Sub claim flowing up against GC, GC claim flowing up against Owner | **Engage counsel.** Consolidation strategy under A201 §15.4.4 / AAA Rule R-7 governs. |
| Architect, EOR, and consultants all named in a defect claim | **Engage counsel** for each design party (PL carriers will assign). Coordination of defense is critical. |
| Owner counterclaim against multiple Contractor parties | **Engage counsel.** Consolidation, joinder, joint defense agreements come into play. |

---

## 8. Defect or latent-condition claim with ongoing damage

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Active leak, structural settlement, ongoing system failure | **Engage counsel** to preserve rights AND **engage forensic expert** to document. Spoliation of evidence is a big risk. |
| Latent defect surfaced post-occupancy | **Engage counsel.** A201 §12.2.2 (1-year correction period) vs. statute of repose (state-specific) become decisive. |

---

## 9. Allegation of professional malpractice

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Owner or Contractor asserts architect / EOR design negligence | **Engage PL carrier counsel.** PL policy notice requirement is typically immediate. |
| Plan-stamp / sealed-document defect | **Engage PL carrier counsel** + state licensing board (depending on jurisdiction reporting requirements). |

---

## 10. Reputational / relationship considerations

Less mechanical, but real. Engage counsel even on smaller disputes when:

- Repeat owner relationship is on the line (next project depends on this dispute resolving cleanly).
- Public profile of the dispute (institutional / public / federal owner; PR exposure).
- Adversary has aggressive counsel already engaged.

---

## When NOT to engage counsel

For symmetry — counsel engagement is not always necessary:

- Small RFI-level scope disputes resolving within field communication.
- Routine change orders within the contractual change-order mechanic (A201 §7).
- Routine submittal disputes resolving within `submittal-review` action stamps.
- Routine pay-app disputes resolving within G702 / G703 negotiation.

When in doubt — **bias toward engaging counsel**. Fee from counsel review on a small dispute is dwarfed by the cost of a procedural error on a medium dispute that could have been avoided.

---

## How this skill phrases the trigger

When any trigger above fires, the memo includes a section titled "Counsel Engagement Recommendation" with:

- **Trigger fired:** [name the specific trigger above].
- **Recommended action:** Engage construction counsel before [next procedural step].
- **Carrier notification (if applicable):** PL / GL / Builder's Risk / surety / cyber.
- **Deadline pressure:** [the deadline that is pushing toward counsel engagement].

If no trigger fires, the memo states explicitly: "No counsel-engagement triggers identified. Procedural next step is [Rung X]; this skill's memo serves as the procedural guide."
