## Disclaimers

This memo is **procedural guidance**, not legal advice. It locates the dispute on the contractual escalation ladder, identifies the next required procedural step, names the controlling deadlines, and flags counsel-engagement triggers. It does **not**:

- Advise on substantive merit of the Claim.
- Recommend settlement strategy or litigation tactics.
- Substitute for licensed construction counsel.
- Quantify damages.
- Decide whether to terminate, sue, or arbitrate.

The escalation-ladder rung-numbering used here is internal to this skill — not a published industry standard. Underlying citations are to AIA A201-2017 (and parallel sections in other contract forms); verify against the executed contract.
