# Policy Types Reference

Standard insurance policy categories on AEC projects, what each covers, who carries it, and heuristic limit tiers by project value. Limits are starting recommendations — the licensed broker's market analysis governs the actual program.

**Tiers:**

| Tier | Construction value |
|------|-------------------|
| Small | < $1M |
| Mid | $1M – $25M |
| Large | $25M – $100M |
| Mega | > $100M |

---

## 1. Professional Liability (PL / E&O)

**What it covers:** Errors, omissions, negligent acts in professional services (design, engineering, project management, construction management, design-build).

**Who carries:**

- Architect (always).
- Engineers (always).
- Design-builder (combined).
- CMAR / agency CM (often required).
- Owner's rep / Owner's project manager (often required by owner).

**Heuristic limits per claim / aggregate:**

| Tier | Architect / Lead Engineer | Specialty Engineer | CMAR / DB |
|------|---------------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Small | $1M / $1M | $500K / $500K | $1M / $2M |
| Mid | $2M / $2M or $5M / $5M | $1M / $1M | $5M / $5M |
| Large | $5M / $10M or $10M / $10M | $2M / $2M | $10M / $10M |
| Mega | $10M / $20M or higher; project-specific PI | $5M / $5M | $25M / $50M or project-specific |

**Required endorsements:** Owner is typically NOT named as additional insured on PL (owner has its own remedies via contract). Some owners require owner's rep / construction manager AI on PL; case-by-case.

**Notes:**

- PL is **claims-made**, not occurrence-based. Tail / extended reporting period (ERP) is critical at firm dissolution or project closeout. Owner often requires 5–10 yr tail.
- Project-Specific Professional Insurance (PSPI / OPPI) is a separate project-bound policy on mega projects, paid by owner.
- PL deductibles are typically per-claim, not annual aggregate.

---

## 2. Commercial General Liability (CGL)

**What it covers:** Bodily injury, property damage, personal & advertising injury arising from operations.

**Who carries:** Owner, GC, every subcontractor.

**Heuristic limits:**

| Tier | Per occurrence / Aggregate | Products-completed ops aggregate |
|------|----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Small | $1M / $2M | $2M |
| Mid | $1M / $2M (subs); $2M / $4M (GC) | $2M (subs); $4M (GC) |
| Large | $2M / $4M with $5M – $10M umbrella | $4M+ |
| Mega | $2M / $4M + $25M+ umbrella tower | Same |

**Required endorsements:**

- **Additional Insured** — Owner + Architect (CG 2010 + CG 2037 forms — both ongoing and completed operations).
- **Primary and Non-Contributory** — owner / architect coverage primary, not pro-rata with their own.
- **Waiver of Subrogation** — in favor of owner + architect.
- **Per-Project Aggregate** — separate aggregate per project, not eroded by other GC projects.

---

## 3. Builder's Risk (Course of Construction)

**What it covers:** Physical loss to the structure during construction (fire, wind, theft, vandalism, water damage).

**Who carries:** Per delivery method — see `delivery_method_insurance_matrix.md`. Typically:

- DBB → Owner (most common) or GC (less common; owner reimburses).
- DB → Design-builder.
- CMAR → Negotiated (often owner).
- IPD → Project (under IPD insurance program).

**Heuristic limits:** Full replacement value of the structure under construction (100% of construction cost + soft costs that should also be covered).

**Required endorsements:**

- **Soft costs** — re-design fees, financing carrying costs, lost rent during reconstruction.
- **Delay in opening / business interruption** — commercial / hospitality projects.
- **Flood + Earthquake** — site-specific.
- **Equipment Breakdown** — for projects with significant MEP equipment.
- **Resultant damage** — covers consequential damage from a covered peril.

**Notes:**

- Coverage typically ends at substantial completion; gap to property insurance can leave owner exposed during punch / TCO.
- Owner-furnished equipment (FF&E) typically not covered unless specifically endorsed.

---

## 4. Workers' Compensation + Employer's Liability

**What it covers:** Statutory benefits for employee injury (WC); liability for employee injury beyond statutory (EL).

**Who carries:** Every employer on the project (GC, subs, owner if owner has employees on site).

**Heuristic limits:**

| Tier | Workers' Comp | Employer's Liability |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| All | Statutory (varies by state) | $1M / $1M / $1M (BI by accident / disease policy / disease per employee) — most projects |
| Large / Mega | Statutory | $1M+ with umbrella excess |

**Notes:**

- **State-specific.** New York, California, Texas, Florida have specific rules. Verify with broker.
- **Texas non-subscriber alternative** — Texas allows employers to opt out of WC and self-insure via alternative benefit plan; rare on construction.
- **Wrap-up programs (OCIP / CCIP)** consolidate WC across all on-site enrolled subs.
- **Owner's Contingent Liability** — if owner has no on-site employees, owner often requires confirmation of all subs' WC certs.

---

## 5. Auto Liability

**What it covers:** Bodily injury and property damage arising from owned, hired, or non-owned autos used in operations.

**Who carries:** GC, every sub, owner if owner uses autos in connection with project.

**Heuristic limits:**

| Tier | Combined Single Limit |
|------|----------------------|
| Small | $1M CSL |
| Mid | $1M CSL |
| Large | $1M CSL with umbrella |
| Mega | $1M CSL with $25M+ umbrella tower |

**Required endorsements:** Symbol 1 (any auto), Owner + Architect as additional insured.

---

## 6. Umbrella / Excess Liability

**What it covers:** Coverage above CGL, Auto, Employer's Liability primary limits.

**Who carries:** GC; subs typically required to carry $5M – $10M umbrella; owner often carries owner's umbrella.

**Heuristic limits:**

| Tier | GC umbrella |
|------|-------------|
| Small | $2M – $5M |
| Mid | $5M – $10M |
| Large | $10M – $25M |
| Mega | $25M – $100M+ tower |

**Notes:**

- "Following form" umbrella mirrors underlying — strict excess.
- "True umbrella" provides broader coverage than underlying — more expensive.

---

## 7. Pollution Legal Liability (PLL)

**What it covers:** Pollution conditions arising from the site (existing contamination, third-party pollution claim).

**Who carries:** Owner, often required on industrial / brownfield / institutional / Phase I-flagged sites.

**Heuristic limits:**

| Site profile | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| Clean Phase I, no concerns | Often not required |
| Phase I findings (recognized environmental conditions) | $1M – $5M project-specific |
| Brownfield / known contamination | $5M – $25M project-specific, plus remediation cost cap |

**Notes:**

- Different from Contractor's Pollution Liability (CPL).
- Often paired with cost-cap insurance on remediation projects.

---

## 8. Contractor's Pollution Liability (CPL)

**What it covers:** Contractor's pollution-causing activities — fuel spill, chemical release during construction, spoils handling.

**Who carries:** GC, hazmat / abatement subs, demolition subs.

**Heuristic limits:**

| Tier | CPL |
|------|-----|
| Small | $1M / $2M |
| Mid | $2M / $4M |
| Large | $5M / $10M |
| Mega | $10M+ project-specific |

---

## 9. Cyber Liability

**What it covers:** Data breach, cyber extortion, business interruption from cyber event, regulatory response.

**Who carries:** Increasingly required on:

- Projects with operational technology (BAS, BMS, building security systems integrated with owner network).
- Federal projects (CMMC requirements flow down).
- Healthcare projects (HIPAA).
- Owner's IT infrastructure projects (data center, hyperscale).

**Heuristic limits:** $1M – $5M typical for most projects; $25M+ for federal / critical infrastructure.

**Notes:** Emerging requirement. Confirm with owner IT / cyber counsel.

---

## 10. Bonds (not insurance, but adjacent)

| Bond | Purpose | Form |
|------|---------|------|
| Bid Bond | Guarantees bidder will execute contract if awarded | AIA A310-2010 |
| Performance Bond | Guarantees performance of contract | AIA A312-2010 |
| Payment Bond | Guarantees payment to subs and suppliers | AIA A312-2010 |

Bonds are surety, not insurance — different product, different markets, different decision. Federal projects > $150K require P+P bonds (Miller Act); state "Little Miller Acts" vary. Refer to broker / surety agent for adequacy.
