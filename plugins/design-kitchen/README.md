# Design Kitchen

Lay out a kitchen around the work that happens in it, and the services.

Lays out a domestic or small commercial kitchen from the sequence of work rather than from a triangle: where things arrive, where they are stored, prepared, cooked, served and cleaned, and what has to be within reach of what. Then works the two things that decide whether the layout survives — where the services can actually go, and what the ventilation demands of the plan and the elevation. Trigger on "kitchen layout", "design the kitchen", "galley or island", "where does the hood vent", "kitchen work triangle", "design-kitchen", or a kitchen with appliances chosen and nowhere settled. Carries no clearances, no aisle widths, no appliance dimensions and no ventilation rates; each comes from the code you quote, the accessibility standard, or the appliance's own data. Commercial kitchens beyond a small back-of-house are a specialist consultant's. It does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
