# Delay Triage

Work out whose delay this is before anyone writes a letter.

Sorts a delay before it becomes a claim: establishes what is actually late against what baseline, whether the delayed activity was ever controlling the completion date, who caused it, and whether the contract makes it compensable, excusable, or neither. Separates the three questions that get merged — is it late, is it critical, and whose is it — because a delay can be real and cost nobody anything. Trigger on "we are behind", "the contractor says we delayed them", "is this a critical delay", "do we owe them time", "delay-triage", or a delay asserted with a letter likely to follow. Carries no contract terms and no float conventions; entitlement comes from the executed agreement you quote. It is not a delay analysis, which is forensic and has a methodology, and it does not replace the scheduler, the attorney, or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
