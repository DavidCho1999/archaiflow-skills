# Site Analysis

Read a site for what it decides, before anything is drawn on it.

Reads a site for the things that will decide the building rather than for a set of diagrams: what is fixed and cannot move, what the approach and the ground do to where a building can sit, where the sun and the weather come from, what the neighbours and the context require, and what has to be found out before anything is committed. Separates observation from inference throughout, and ends with the list of what is still unknown and who answers it. Trigger on "site analysis", "read this site", "what does the site tell us", "first visit to the site", "site-analysis", or a new site with nothing decided yet. Carries no climate data, no zoning limits and no soil values; `climate-zone-mapper`, `seismic-lookup`, `wind-snow-lookup` and `zoning-summary` collect those and this reads what they found for what it means. It is not a survey and does not replace the surveyor, the civil engineer, or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
