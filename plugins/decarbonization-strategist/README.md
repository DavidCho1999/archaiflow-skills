# Carbon Roadmap

Plan a building's path to lower carbon over 5-15 years.

Builds a phased decarbonisation roadmap for a building over five to fifteen years, covering operational carbon (envelope, systems, electrification, on-site renewables, off-site supply) and embodied carbon (materials, reuse against new build), sequenced against the mandate dates and incentives the user names. Trigger on "decarbonization roadmap", "decarb plan", "net zero strategy", "electrification roadmap", "embodied carbon plan", "carbon reduction plan", a pasted owner brief with a carbon target, or an energy baseline with a jurisdiction. Works in any jurisdiction. Every mandate date, cap, threshold, and incentive value comes from the user; the skill supplies the sequencing method and never a regulatory figure. Produces a roadmap memo, not an energy model, not a life cycle assessment, and not a binding commitment.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
