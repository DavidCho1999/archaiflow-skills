## NYC — Local Law 97 (LL97)

**Citation:** NYC Admin Code §28-320; implementing rules at 1 RCNY §103-14 (Article 321 — 2024 Rules). Verify current version with NYC Department of Buildings Sustainability + DEP / OFFICE OF BUILDING ENERGY.

**Applies to:** Buildings ≥ 25,000 gross sf in NYC, with limited exceptions (§28-320.1.1).

**Building emissions limits:**

- **Compliance Period 1 (2024–2029):** First emissions limits effective. Building-class-specific tCO2e/sf cap (Table 1 of Article 321). **Cite specific cap per building class from Article 321 Table 1 — do not recall from memory; the table is published by NYC DOB.**
- **Compliance Period 2 (2030–2034):** Significantly lower caps; building-decarbonization moves required for most buildings to comply. **Cite specific cap from Article 321 Table 1 (or successor table).**
- **Compliance Period 3 (2035–2039) and Period 4 (2040–2049):** Further ratchets toward 2050 80% reduction target. **Cite specific cap.**

**Penalty structure:**

- §28-320.6: Civil penalty calculated as $268 / tCO2e over the cap, times the number of years in the period. **Verify current per-tonne penalty with NYC DOB; legislatively set but adjustment possible.**

**Compliance pathways:**

- Direct emissions reduction (envelope, HVAC, electrification, on-site renewables).
- Beneficial Electrification Credits.
- Renewable Energy Credits (RECs) — limited substitution per Article 321 (specific limits — do not recall).
- Greenhouse Gas Offsets — limited substitution per Article 321 (specific limits).
- Building Energy Storage System (BESS) credit.

**Reporting:**

- LL84 (NYC Admin Code §28-309) — annual benchmarking submission; basis for LL97 emissions calculation.
- LL97 annual emissions report.

**Adjacent NYC mandates:**

- LL87 (NYC Admin Code §28-308) — decennial energy audit + retro-commissioning.
- LL88 (NYC Admin Code §28-310) — lighting + sub-metering retrofit by 2025 (or applicable deadline).
- FISP (1 RCNY §103-04) — periodic façade inspection; intersects with envelope retrofit timing.

**Re-verification:** Quarterly during active LL97 rulemaking (2025–2030).

## Boston — Building Emissions Reduction and Disclosure Ordinance (BERDO 2.0)

**Citation:** Boston City Code §7-2.2 (amended 2021); implementing rules from City of Boston Environment Department. Verify current version.

**Applies to:**

- Commercial buildings ≥ 20,000 sf (different threshold for residential).
- Residential buildings ≥ 15 units.

**Performance periods:**

- Performance Period 1 starts 2025 for largest buildings; phases earlier for smaller buildings through 2030.
- Targets ratchet through 2050 (net-zero target).

**Compliance pathways:**

- Direct emissions reduction.
- Alternative Compliance Payments (ACP) — verify current $/tCO2e rate with Boston ED.
- RECs / offsets — limited.

**Reporting:**

- Annual benchmarking submission (predates BERDO 2.0; carried over).
- Annual emissions report.

**Re-verification:** Annual minimum; quarterly during active BERDO rulemaking.

## Washington DC — Building Energy Performance Standards (BEPS)

**Citation:** DC Law 22-257 (Clean Energy DC Omnibus Amendment Act, 2018); implementing rules at 19 DCMR §1500. Verify current version with DOEE.

**Applies to:**

- Buildings ≥ 50,000 sf — Tier 1.
- Smaller buildings phase in over time.

**Performance standards:**

- Site EUI thresholds set by building type (median ENERGY STAR Score).
- 5-year performance periods.

**Compliance pathways:**

- Direct EUI reduction (most buildings achieve via envelope + HVAC + electrification).
- Alternative compliance: payment, on-site solar, etc.

**Re-verification:** Annual minimum.

## CA — Title 24 Part 6 (Building Energy Efficiency Standards)

**Citation:** CCR Title 24 Part 6. Verify current cycle with CA Energy Commission.

**Applies to:** New construction + qualifying alterations / additions.

**Cycles:**

- 2022 cycle effective Jan 2023.
- **2025 cycle effective Jan 2026** — significant electrification + heat-pump requirements.
- Subsequent cycles every 3 years.

**Compliance pathways:**

- Prescriptive (component-by-component requirements).
- Performance (whole-building energy modeling against budget).
- Tier 1 / Tier 2 within CALGreen (Title 24 Part 11).

**Adjacent CA mandates:**

- CA Title 24 Part 11 (CALGreen) — green building code; embodied-carbon provisions.
- CA SB 253 (Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act) — corporate scope 1+2 reporting starts 2026; scope 3 starts 2027.
- CA SB 261 (Climate Risk Disclosure) — biennial climate financial risk reporting.
- CA AB 32 / SB 32 / Cap-and-Trade — statewide carbon cap.

**Re-verification:** Annual minimum; cycle-change triggers full re-verification.

## NY State — Energy Conservation Construction Code (ECCC)

**Citation:** 19 NYCRR Part 1240. Verify current cycle.

**Applies to:** New construction + qualifying alterations.

**Cycles:** Triennial (typically lags IECC by 1–2 cycles).

## WA — Clean Buildings Performance Standard

**Citation:** RCW 19.27A.210; WAC 194-50.

**Applies to:**

- Tier 1: Buildings ≥ 50,000 sf.
- Tier 2: Buildings ≥ 20,000 sf — phasing in.

**Performance standard:** Site EUI thresholds by building type; 10-year compliance period.

---

## Denver — Energize Denver

**Citation:** Denver City Code Article XIII.

**Applies to:** Buildings ≥ 25,000 sf (different threshold for multifamily).

**Performance standards:** EUI thresholds; phased through 2030.

---

## Canada — Vancouver Zero Emissions Building Plan (ZEBP)

**Citation:** City of Vancouver ZEBP, adopted 2016, updated periodically.

**Applies to:** New construction.

**Targets:**

- Energy-use intensity (TEUI), thermal-energy demand intensity (TEDI), greenhouse-gas intensity (GHGI) thresholds ratcheting through 2030.

---

## Canada — Toronto Green Standard (TGS) v4

**Citation:** Toronto City Council adopted; updated periodically.

**Applies to:** New construction.

**Tiers:**

- Tier 1 (mandatory minimum for site-plan approval).
- Tier 2 / 3 / 4 (voluntary; eligible for development-charge refunds).

---

## Federal — Executive Order 14057 + GSA P-100

**Citation:** EO 14057 (2021); GSA P-100 Facilities Standards.

**Applies to:** Federal buildings.

**Targets:**

- 65% reduction in scope 1+2 emissions from federal buildings by 2030.
- Net-zero federal buildings by 2045.

**Adjacent:**

- Federal Buy Clean Initiative — embodied carbon thresholds for federally-procured concrete, steel, asphalt, flat glass.

## How to use this reference

1. Identify the jurisdiction(s) applicable to the project.
2. Confirm building meets the threshold (sf or unit count).
3. Identify the next deadline + the controlling cap / target.
4. Direct the user to the primary source for current cap / target / fine value.
5. **Do not recall the specific cap or fine number from memory** — the roadmap memo cites the source by section, and either:
   - Pulls the current value from the primary source URL at memo-creation time, or
   - States explicitly "verify current cap with [primary source]" if not pulled.

The roadmap pathing in `operational_carbon_pathway.md` and `embodied_carbon_pathway.md` works backward from the binding mandate deadline.
