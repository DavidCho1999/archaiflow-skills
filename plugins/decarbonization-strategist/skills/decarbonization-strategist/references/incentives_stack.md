# Incentives Stack — Federal / State / Utility

Federal, state, and utility incentives that fund decarbonization moves. **Cite from this file rather than recalling rates from memory — IRA provisions and state programs change frequently.** Direct user to IRS guidance + tax counsel for federal incentive application; direct to utility / state agency for state + utility programs.

---

## Federal — Inflation Reduction Act (IRA, Pub. L. 117-169, Aug 2022)

### §13301 — Energy Efficient Commercial Buildings Deduction (§179D)

**What:** Tax deduction for installation of energy-efficient property (envelope, HVAC + hot water systems, interior lighting) in commercial buildings.

**Eligible:** Commercial building owners; tax-exempt entities (governments, schools) may allocate to designer (architect / engineer / DB).

**Deduction structure:** Per-sf deduction tied to whole-building energy reduction vs. ASHRAE 90.1 reference. Bonus multiplier for prevailing-wage + apprenticeship compliance.

**Verify:** Current per-sf deduction rate, energy-reduction thresholds, prevailing-wage requirements with current IRS Notice (e.g., Notice 2023-65, Notice 2024-XX successors). **Do not recall specific dollar figures from memory.**

**Strategy:** For tax-exempt projects (federal, state, K-12, hospital), confirm allocation strategy before bidding; designer (often architect) may receive the deduction.

### §13303 — New Energy Efficient Home Credit (§45L)

**What:** Per-unit tax credit for new + substantially reconstructed residential units meeting ENERGY STAR or Zero Energy Ready Home (ZERH) tier requirements.

**Eligible:** Builder / developer of qualifying residential units (single-family + multifamily up to 3 stories; multifamily ≥ 4 stories under separate amendment).

**Verify:** Current per-unit credit by tier, ENERGY STAR / ZERH version requirements with current IRS Notice. **Do not recall specific dollar figures from memory.**

### §13101 — Investment Tax Credit (§48)

**What:** ITC for solar PV, solar thermal, fuel cells, geothermal, small wind, microturbines, CHP, BESS (post-2022).

**Base + bonus structure:**

- Base credit (verify current %).
- **Domestic-content bonus** — additional % if specific steel / iron / manufactured-product domestic-content thresholds met. **Cite IRS Notice 2023-38 + successors for current thresholds.**
- **Energy-community bonus** — additional % if facility located in qualifying energy community (coal closure, fossil-employment census tract). **Cite IRS Notice 2023-29 + successors.**
- **Low-income community bonus** (limited cap) — additional % if facility located in qualifying low-income community.
- **Prevailing-wage + apprenticeship requirement** — failure to comply reduces base credit by 5×; compliance maintains full credit. **Cite IRS Notice 2022-61 + successors.**

**Verify:** Current base rate + each bonus rate.

### §13701 / §13702 — Clean Electricity (§45Y / §48E)

**What:** Technology-neutral PTC / ITC for clean electricity facilities placed in service after 2024.

**Verify:** Current rates + bonus structure.

## Utility programs

Local utilities offer per-kWh / per-therm avoided rebates for:

- Heat pumps (per ton or per kBtu).
- Variable-speed equipment + ECM motors.
- Lighting + controls.
- BAS optimization + recommissioning.
- Demand response (peak-load shifting).
- Net metering / virtual net metering for on-site PV.
- BESS + grid-services (varies; some utilities pay for grid-stabilization services).

**Verify with the project's specific utility — programs vary sharply by utility, even within a state.**

## Stacking strategy

**General principle:** Federal + state + utility incentives can typically stack, but with constraints:

- IRA §48 ITC + state utility rebate: typically allowed; check for "anti-stacking" language in state program.
- IRA §179D + state efficiency rebate: typically allowed.
- IRA §13301 / §13303 + state homeowner rebate: typically allowed for residential.
- HEEHRA / HOMES: state-administered, ineligible if other federal incentive claimed for same scope (verify with state energy office).
- §48 ITC bonus credits stack with each other (domestic-content + energy-community + prevailing-wage all available).

**Application strategy:**

- IRA: tax counsel + cost segregation specialist on each project > $5M.
- §179D allocation for tax-exempt: negotiate before bid.
- State + utility: typically pre-application before equipment install; some programs require commissioning report.
- Carbon credits: sell only if not needed for mandate compliance (LL97, BERDO).

## Anti-patterns

- **Quoting specific IRA rates from memory.** Rates have changed 2022 enactment → IRS guidance → corrections; cite current IRS Notice.
- **Assuming domestic-content / energy-community / prevailing-wage bonus auto-applies.** Each requires specific documentation; verify before pricing.
- **Treating §179D as automatic.** Requires whole-building energy modeling vs. ASHRAE 90.1 reference; modeling cost is non-trivial; viable only when energy reduction is significant.
- **Treating §45L as automatic.** Requires ENERGY STAR / ZERH certification; certification cost is per-unit.
- **Stacking REC sales with mandate compliance.** RECs sold = grid-emissions claim taken by buyer; cannot also count as your building's reduction.
- **Failing to coordinate prevailing-wage + apprenticeship compliance with GC procurement.** Compliance is project-wide for all eligible labor; monitoring + reporting is significant overhead.
