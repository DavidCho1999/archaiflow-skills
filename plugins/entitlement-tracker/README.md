# Entitlement Tracker

Track a discretionary approval from application through hearings to conditions.

Keeps one dated record of a discretionary land use approval, which behaves nothing like a permit: the decision-maker is a body that meets on a calendar, the outcome is negotiated rather than checked, and the conditions attached at the end become obligations the project carries for its life. Logs every submission, every hearing, every commitment made in a room, and every condition with what discharges it and who owns it. Trigger on "entitlement tracker", "track the rezoning", "planning approval log", "what did we commit to at the hearing", "conditions of approval", "entitlement-tracker", or a discretionary application handed over to keep track of. Carries no process, no timelines and no hearing calendars; all come from the jurisdiction. It does not track building permits — `permit-tracker` does — and does not replace the land use attorney, the planner, or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
