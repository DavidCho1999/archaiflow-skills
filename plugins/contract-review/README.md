# Contract Review

Read an owner-drafted agreement for what it moves onto you.

Reads an owner-drafted or heavily amended professional services agreement for the provisions that shift risk onto the architect, working in the order the money moves rather than the order the document is written: standard of care, indemnity, limitation of liability, insurance, scope creep, payment and termination, ownership, and the dispute path. Reports each finding with the clause quoted, what it changes from the industry-standard position, whether it is insurable, and whether it is usually negotiable. Trigger on "review this contract", "the client sent their own agreement", "is this indemnity clause standard", "can we sign this", "contract-review", or an agreement handed over before signing. Carries no standard-form text and no legal positions; comparisons to a standard form require you to supply it. It is not legal advice and does not replace the attorney, the broker, or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
