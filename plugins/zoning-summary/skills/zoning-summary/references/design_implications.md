# Constraint → Design Move

Each zoning constraint is also an early-design instruction. Use this when filling the worksheet so every line item carries a one-sentence implication, not just a number.

---

## High FAR / FSI (>5)
- Implication: Tower or stacked typology. Core efficiency drives plate size; section diagram dominates design.

## Low FAR / FSI (<2)
- Implication: Low-rise spread. Plan logic and outdoor rooms become the project; section is secondary.

## Tight side setbacks (<5 ft / <1.5 m)
- Implication: Treat side walls as blind party walls; daylight comes from front, rear, or skylights/light wells.

## Generous front setback / front yard
- Implication: Front yard becomes the public-private threshold. Landscape and entry sequence matter more than facade.

## Sky exposure plane / daylight plane / angular plane
- Implication: Upper-floor stepbacks. Terraces fall out naturally; design them as program, not residual.

## Height limit in stories AND feet/metres
- Implication: Floor-to-floor budget tightens. Trades off ceiling height vs. mechanical depth — decide early.

## Historic / Heritage Conservation District overlay
- Implication: Material palette, window proportions, and roof form are not free variables. Lead with context analysis.

## Floodplain overlay
- Implication: First-floor elevation requirement may push the building up — entry sequence, ramp/stair, and mechanical placement re-think.

## Transit overlay / MTSA with reduced parking
- Implication: Free up ground floor and basement for active program. Reconsider whether parking is needed at all.

## Inclusionary zoning trigger
- Implication: Unit mix changes — affordable units affect plan modules and mechanical zoning.

## Conditional use / discretionary review / Committee of Adjustment
- Implication: Add 6-12 months to schedule; design must be community-presentable, not just permit-ready, by an earlier date.

## Form-based code (active ground floor, transparency %)
- Implication: Ground-floor section, ceiling height, and glazing ratio become regulatory, not stylistic.

## Required parking minimum (high)
- Implication: Below-grade parking levels become a cost driver. Test whether reducing units or shifting use can drop a level.

## Shadow study requirement
- Implication: Mass placement and stepbacks must be tested against adjacent open space at solstice/equinox before locking massing.

## Section 37 / Community Benefits Charge (Toronto)
- Implication: Density bonus is paid for in cash or community space. Decide early whether to push for the bonus or design within base FSI.

---

## Cross-region principle

A constraint is only a design driver when it is *binding*. If FAR/FSI is generous and the program doesn't need it, FAR is not the design driver — the tighter constraint (height, setback, parking, angular plane) is. Identify which 2-3 constraints actually bind, and design from those.
