# Jargon Glossary — Tech ↔ Client

Map technical architectural / engineering terms to lived-experience equivalents. Use when translating tech → client. Reverse direction: when client uses a sensory phrase, decompose into the right-hand technical terms.

When a term is missing, ask the user once rather than inventing.

## Thermal & envelope

| Technical                       | Lived-experience equivalent                                                       |
|---------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| R-27 wall (U-0.037 Btu/h·ft²·°F) | "Interior walls stay at room temperature even on a 14°F winter day"              |
| 4-inch rigid (XPS) insulation   | "Heavy-duty insulation — blocks more cold than standard wall insulation"           |
| Passive House (PHIUS+ 2021)     | "PHIUS-certified near-zero-heating-bill standard, the North American Passive House" |
| Condensation                    | "Water beads on the wall — caused by poor insulation or ventilation, leads to mold" |
| Thermal bridge                  | "A break in the wall where heat escapes through framing or steel"                  |
| Air tightness / blower-door     | "How well the building keeps drafts and dust from leaking through"                |
| HRV / ERV (Heat Recovery Ventilator) | "Auto-swaps stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air, recovering most of the heat" |

## Cost / budget framing

When the client sees a number:
- "$500,000 cost increase needed" → keep verbatim. NEVER soften to "a small adjustment".
- Add ratio context: "(about X% of the construction budget)".
- Add time-to-payoff if relevant: "Recouped in 5-7 years through lower heating bills".

## Decomposing client wishes (client → tech)

Common client phrases and their technical decomposition:

| Client phrase             | Technical decomposition                                                                          |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| "Warm and cozy feel"       | 2700-3000K CCT, wood veneer / fabric, ceiling height ~8 ft, warm-tone finishes, rugs               |
| "Bright and open feel"     | 4000K+ CCT, ceiling height 9'-2"+, wide windows (min 6 ft), light-tone finishes, minimal furniture |
| "Luxurious feel"           | Natural stone or real wood veneer, flush trim (hidden details), dimming, solid hardware           |
| "Eco-friendly feel"        | Low-VOC certified finishes, natural ventilation, integrated planting, reclaimed/recycled materials, passive design |
| "Modern feel"              | Monolithic finishes, hidden baseboards / reveals, simple palette (≤3 materials), clean lines, matte finishes |
| "Mid-century" feel         | Warm woods (walnut, teak), low-slung furniture, clerestory / picture windows, brick or stone hearth, exposed beams |
| "Industrial" feel          | Exposed concrete or brick, exposed steel / ductwork, large warehouse-style windows, polished concrete floors |

When the client phrase is too vague (e.g. "make it pretty"), ask one clarifying question rather than guessing.
