# Design Entry

Work the entry as a sequence from the street to the second door.

Works the entrance as a continuous sequence rather than as a door: how it is found from the street, what happens on the way in, what the threshold has to hold at once, and where somebody stands when they arrive and nobody is expecting them. Names the four things that meet at a doorway and cannot all be optimised — level access, weather, energy, and security — and says which one this project lets win. Trigger on "entrance design", "the entry sequence", "where is the front door", "vestibule", "the entrance is hard to find", "design-entry", or an entrance that is a door on a plan and nothing more. Carries no dimensions, no threshold limits and no requirements; accessible thresholds and vestibule requirements are code and standards you quote. It does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
