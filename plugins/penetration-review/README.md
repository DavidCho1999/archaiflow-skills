# Penetration Review

Find every hole through an assembly and say what closes it, and to what.

Works through every penetration of a rated, acoustic or structural assembly and asks the three questions that get answered separately and should be answered together: what performance the assembly has to keep, what physically closes the hole, and whether a listed or tested system exists for that penetrant in that assembly. Builds the register across disciplines, since penetrations are made by trades who never see each other's drawings. Trigger on "penetration review", "firestopping check", "what closes this hole", "acoustic penetrations", "sleeve schedule review", "penetration-review", or an MEP set laid over the rated wall plan. Carries no listings, no ratings and no acoustic values; the required performance comes from your code analysis and every closure is a listed or tested system you cite. For the assembly's own continuity use `design-fire-barrier`; for air and water use `air-barrier-trace`. It does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
