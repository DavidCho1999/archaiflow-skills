## Contract instrument required

Once owner accepts, items roll into:

- **G701 (Change Order)** — items changing contract sum and/or time.
- **G710 (ASI)** — equivalent substitutions, no sum/time change.
- **G714 (CCD)** — items requiring direction before price/time agreement (typically long-lead).

Reference: AIA A201-2017 §7. See `references/aia_contracts_quick_ref.md`.

---

## Disclaimers

This memo is the Architect's review of proposed VE items per AIA B101-2017 §3.6.1.5 (review for conformance with design concept). It is **not**:

- A directive to the Contractor (no work proceeds until G701 / G710 / G714 issued).
- A cost validation (the $ savings stated are as quoted by the CM/GC; the Architect does not estimate).
- A code-compliance certification (any item touching egress, fire-resistance, accessibility, or envelope thermal requires re-review by `code-compliance-review` after acceptance).
- A binding owner decision (owner makes the accept/reject call).

Six-axis classification framework is internal to this review skill; ASTM E1699 publishes the underlying VE Job Plan methodology.
