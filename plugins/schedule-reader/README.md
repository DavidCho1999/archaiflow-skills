# Schedule Reader

Read an engineer's schedule for what it means to the architecture.

Reads any engineer's or consultant's schedule — mechanical equipment, plumbing fixtures, electrical panels, lighting, fire protection devices, structural members — and translates it into the architectural consequences: what has to fit somewhere, what needs a room, what needs access, what needs a route, what lands on a wall or a ceiling, and what weighs something the structure has to carry. Trigger on "read this equipment schedule", "what does this mechanical schedule mean for us", "they sent a schedule", "schedule-reader", or any consultant's schedule handed over. Carries no dimensions, capacities or clearances; every figure comes from the schedule or the product data. Not to be confused with `schedule-review`, which reads a contractor's construction programme — a different document entirely. `explain-panel-schedule` goes deeper on one electrical panel. It does not check the engineering and does not replace the engineers or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
