# Circulation Study

Trace each journey through the plan and find where two that should not meet do.

Studies circulation as a set of named journeys rather than as corridor area: establishes who moves through the building and why, traces each journey door to door, finds the places two journeys cross that should not, and reports where a route depends on something being unlocked, staffed or explained. Separates the circulation a plan needs from the circulation it happens to have. Trigger on "circulation study", "trace the routes", "do these flows cross", "clean and dirty separation", "public and staff routes", "wayfinding through the plan", "circulation-study", or a plan handed over with a question about how people move through it. Carries no corridor widths, no efficiency ratios and no separation requirements; egress widths and distances belong to `design-egress` and accessibility to the accessibility standard you quote. It does not lay out a plan and does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
