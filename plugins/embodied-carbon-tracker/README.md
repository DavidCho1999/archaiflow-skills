# Embodied Carbon Tracker

Track embodied carbon by stage against factors you supply.

Keeps one dated record of a project's embodied carbon as the design changes: quantities from your own take-off, factors from the data source you name, and the result kept per element and per life-cycle stage rather than as one number, because a single figure hides which decision moved it. Records the boundary and the data vintage on every entry, since two projects' figures are comparable only when both are stated. Trigger on "embodied carbon", "track the carbon on this design", "whole life carbon", "EPD", "upfront carbon", "A1-A3", "embodied-carbon-tracker", or a material change with a question about its carbon. Carries no emission factors and no benchmarks; every factor comes from an EPD or a database you name, with its vintage. `decarbonization-strategist` plans the operational path over years; this counts what the materials carry. It is not an LCA and does not replace the sustainability consultant.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
