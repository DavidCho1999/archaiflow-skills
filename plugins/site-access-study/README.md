# Site Access Study

Work out how vehicles, services, and fire access reach the site.

Studies everything that has to reach a site and finds where two of them want the same frontage: lists every arrival — people, cars, deliveries, refuse, fire apparatus, utilities, moving vans, coaches — traces each from the public way to where it has to end up, and reports the conflicts, the curb cuts that will not be granted, and the frontage the project has already spent. Trigger on "site access study", "how does the truck get in", "where does the fire access go", "can we get a curb cut here", "loading and refuse access", "site-access-study", or a site plan with a question about how anything reaches it. Carries no dimensions, no turning radii, no fire apparatus requirements and no curb cut standards; all come from the jurisdiction, the fire department and the utility. It does no traffic engineering and no swept path analysis, and does not replace the civil engineer or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
