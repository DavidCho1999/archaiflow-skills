# Budget Reconciliation

Close the gap between the programme and the budget, and price what goes.

Works the conversation every project has and most handle badly: the design costs more than the budget. Establishes first what each number actually is, since a client's budget often excludes things an estimate includes and half the gap disappears there. Then sorts the ways of closing it into the four that behave differently — less area, less quality, less scope, more money — and prices each move in what it costs the design rather than only in what it saves. Trigger on "we are over budget", "the estimate came back high", "what do we cut", "value engineering at design stage", "the client's budget will not cover this", "budget-reconciliation", or an estimate set against a budget with a gap between them. Carries no costs, no rates and no benchmarks; every figure comes from the estimator, the contractor, or a source you name. `value-engineering-advisor` sorts a cost-cutting list during construction; this is the design-stage conversation. It does not estimate and does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
