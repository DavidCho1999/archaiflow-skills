# Change Order Review

Work through a change's scope, cost, and time before it is signed.

Reviews a proposed change order against a fixed checklist and keeps a running change log in docs/construction/change-log.md. Trigger on "change order", "CO review", "change proposal", "PCO", "COP", "cost of this change", "change log", "change-order-review", or a pasted change proposal. The checklist asks whether the scope is described, the cost is broken down, the time impact is stated, the contractual basis is named, and the markup matches what the contract allows as you quote it. The skill supplies no judgment of fair price and no contract terms from memory; clause text comes from you or explain-aia, and price sanity is lookup-cost's separate job. The log carries every change with the running total against the original contract sum, arithmetic shown. Approval is the owner's and architect's act, not this skill's.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
