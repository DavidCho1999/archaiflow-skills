# Design Accessible Restroom

Lay out an accessible restroom from clearances that overlap, not a checklist.

Lays out an accessible restroom or toilet room as a set of overlapping clearances rather than as a list of items to tick: establishes which clearances may overlap and which may not from the standard you quote, places the fixtures in the order that constrains the plan most, works the door swing against the clear floor space it eats, and reports every dimension as a question rather than an answer. Trigger on "accessible restroom layout", "toilet room won't fit", "ADA bathroom", "single-user accessible washroom", "turning circle in the toilet", "design-accessible-restroom", or a toilet room plan with a request for whether it works. Carries no dimensions, no clearances and no reach ranges; every one is text you quote from the standard your jurisdiction adopts, which is not always the one you expect. For checking one clearance already dimensioned use `/test-ada`; for a project-wide record use `ada-tracker`. It does not replace the accessibility consultant or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
