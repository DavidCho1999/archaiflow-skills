# Design Mechanical Room

Size a plant room for the equipment, the air, and getting it back out again.

Lays out a mechanical plant room from the equipment schedule the engineer gives you, and adds the four things that make it bigger than the equipment: the service clearances each unit needs, the route to get equipment in during construction and out at replacement, the air the room itself has to take in and exhaust, and the connections that leave it. Reports the required footprint and height against what is drawn. Trigger on "size the mechanical room", "plant room layout", "will the AHU fit", "how do we get the chiller in", "mechanical room design", "design-mechanical-room", or an equipment schedule handed over with a room to put it in. Carries no equipment dimensions, no clearances, no ventilation rates and no code requirements; all come from the engineer, the manufacturer and the code text you quote. It does not select or size equipment and does not replace the MEP engineer or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
