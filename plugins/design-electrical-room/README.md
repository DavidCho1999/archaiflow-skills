# Design Electrical Room

Lay out an electrical room around the clearances that cannot be built on.

Lays out an electrical, communications or service entrance room around the working clearances that surround the equipment rather than around the equipment itself, because those clearances are code, they are volumes, and they appear on no architectural drawing by default. Works the utility's own requirements separately from the code's, since a service entrance room is designed to the utility's standard and they will refuse a room that meets the code and not their manual. Trigger on "electrical room layout", "how big does the switchgear room need to be", "working clearance in front of the panel", "service entrance room", "transformer vault", "design-electrical-room", or an electrical equipment schedule with a room to put it in. Carries no clearance dimensions, no equipment sizes and no utility requirements. It does not do electrical design and does not replace the electrical engineer or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
