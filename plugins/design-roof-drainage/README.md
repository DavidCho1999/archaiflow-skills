# Design Roof Drainage

Get water off the roof, and say where it goes when a drain blocks.

Works roof drainage as the architectural half of a problem the plumbing engineer sizes: where the falls come from and what they cost the build-up, where the drains land against the structure and the spaces below, where the secondary drainage goes and whether anyone would notice it running, and what the roof weighs if the primary drains block. Trigger on "roof drainage", "where do the drains go", "tapered insulation layout", "overflow scuppers", "is the roof ponding", "design-roof-drainage", or a roof plan with a question about falls. Carries no drain sizes, no rainfall intensities, no slope minimums and no code requirements; sizing is the plumbing engineer's against the code you quote, and the ponding load is the structural engineer's. `design-roof-assembly` builds the stack and names this as out of its scope. It does not replace the engineers or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
