# Facade Access Study

Work out how every face of the envelope gets reached, cleaned, and replaced.

Works out how every part of a building's envelope will be reached after it is built: cleaned, inspected, repaired, and, for glass, replaced. Inventories the envelope by zone, holds candidate access methods against each one — reach from grade, rope descent, davits, a building maintenance unit, interior access — and reports what each method demands of the design now: roof space, structure, anchors, parapets, and ground kept clear. Trigger on "facade access", "how do we clean this", "window washing", "BMU", "davits", "rope access", "facade-access-study", "how does the glass get replaced", or a tall or complex envelope with no maintenance story. The zoning of the envelope and the demands table are the skill's method; every load, every regulation, and every equipment figure comes from elsewhere: anchor and davit loads are the structural engineer's, the rules for suspended access are regulatory text the user quotes, and reach figures come from the equipment's own data.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
