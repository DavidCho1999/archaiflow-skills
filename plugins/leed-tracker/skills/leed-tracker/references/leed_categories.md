# LEED v4.1 BD+C: NC — Category Structure

The LEED v4.1 BD+C: New Construction rating system has 8 categories totaling 110 possible points.

| Code | Category | Max Points | Role |
|------|----------|-----------:|------|
| **IP** | Integrative Process | 1 | Pre-design coordination |
| **LT** | Location and Transportation | 16 | Where the building sits and how people get there |
| **SS** | Sustainable Sites | 10 | Site-scale environmental impact |
| **WE** | Water Efficiency | 11 | Indoor + outdoor water use |
| **EA** | Energy and Atmosphere | 33 | Highest-weighted category — modeled energy performance dominates |
| **MR** | Materials and Resources | 13 | Material selection, embodied carbon, waste |
| **EQ** | Indoor Environmental Quality | 16 | Air, light, acoustic, thermal comfort |
| **IN** | Innovation | 6 | Bonus for exceeding requirements / pilot credits |
| **RP** | Regional Priority | 4 | Bonus for credits relevant to project's region |
| | **Total** | **110** | |

## Certification thresholds

| Level | Points required (verbatim from USGBC v4.1) |
|-------|--------------------------------------------|
| Certified | 40–49 |
| Silver | 50–59 |
| Gold | 60–79 |
| Platinum | 80+ |

## Prerequisites (P) vs credits

Each category has both **prerequisites** (mandatory, no points) and **credits** (optional, points-bearing). All prerequisites must be met for *any* certification level. Prerequisites are listed with `P` flag in `leed_v41_bdc_nc_points.md`.

## Where the points come from at high level

A typical mid-range project hits Silver/Gold by:

- All prerequisites met (prereq compliance)
- ~15–20 points from EA (energy modeling + commissioning + renewables)
- ~10 points from LT (compact development, transit, density)
- ~6–10 points from MR (Building Product Disclosure)
- ~6 points from WE (indoor + outdoor)
- ~6–10 points from EQ (low-emitting materials, daylight, IAQ)
- 1 IP + 4–6 IN + 1–4 RP

This breakdown is offered as orientation, not as a target. Actual point pursuit varies dramatically by project type, climate, budget, and team experience.
