# Program Reader

Read a brief or programme document for what its numbers rest on.

Reads any brief or programme document — a client's schedule of accommodation, a spreadsheet from a user group, a consultant's brief, a previous architect's programme — and establishes what its numbers actually are before anybody designs against them: which are measured, which are standards, which are wishes, what area basis they are on, whether the total is the sum of the parts, and what the document counts that a building would not. Trigger on "read this brief", "they sent a schedule of accommodation", "what does this programme actually say", "is this area realistic", "program-reader", or a brief handed over. Carries no area standards and no benchmarks; the basis comes from the document or from `area-measurement`. `brief-review` interrogates a brief for gaps and contradictions; this reads one for what its figures rest on, and it runs first. It does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
