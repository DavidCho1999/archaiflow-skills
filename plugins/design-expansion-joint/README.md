# Design Expansion Joint

Place movement joints and carry the control layers across a gap that moves.

Works movement in the enclosure as two questions rather than one: where the joints have to be, given the movements you can name and where they accumulate, and then how each of the four control layers (water, air, vapor, thermal) crosses a gap that opens and closes for the life of the building. Trigger on "expansion joint", "movement joint", "control joint", "where do the joints go", "building separation joint", "how does the air barrier cross a joint", "design-expansion-joint", or a long facade or a structural break named with a request for how the enclosure handles movement. Supplies the reasoning about movement sources, accumulation and joint hierarchy, and the questions each crossing has to answer; supplies no joint spacing, no movement coefficient, no joint width, no code requirement and no proprietary product. It does no structural or thermal calculation and does not replace the enclosure consultant or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
