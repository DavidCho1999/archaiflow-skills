# Existing Conditions Survey

Record what is actually there, and mark what you did not verify.

Plans and records a measured survey of an existing building so that every dimension later designed against carries its provenance: what to capture before going, how to close a measurement so errors are caught on site rather than in the office, and the discipline of marking every line as measured, taken from a record drawing, or assumed. Produces the record and the list of what still has to be opened up or verified. Trigger on "measure the existing building", "as-built survey", "existing conditions", "we only have the old drawings", "what do I measure on the site visit", "existing-conditions-survey", or a renovation starting with no reliable information. Carries no tolerances and no survey standards; the level of accuracy needed comes from what the drawings will be used for. It is not a land survey — `survey-review` reads one — and not a structural, hazardous materials, or condition assessment, each of which is a specialist's and is named as a hand-off.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
