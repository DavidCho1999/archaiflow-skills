# Mockup Review

Review a built mockup against what the documents actually asked for.

Reviews a built enclosure mockup, visual or performance, against the documents rather than against how it looks: establishes what this mockup was built to prove before anyone walks up to it, works the observation in a fixed order so the buildable-but-wrong conditions get seen, separates what the mockup demonstrated from what it merely did not contradict, and records what becomes the accepted standard for the rest of the work. Trigger on "mockup review", "reviewing the visual mockup", "performance mockup results", "what do we check at the mockup", "mockup punch list", "mockup-review", or a mockup photographed or described with a request for what to look at. Carries no tolerances, no test criteria and no acceptance thresholds; every one of those comes from the specification. It is not a test report, does not witness testing, does not accept or reject on the Architect's behalf, and does not replace the enclosure consultant or the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
