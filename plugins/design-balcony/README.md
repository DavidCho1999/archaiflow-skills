# Design Balcony

Carry structure, water, and insulation past the wall without losing any.

Works a balcony, terrace or projecting deck as the place where the envelope is deliberately broken: something structural passes through the insulation, the waterproofing has to turn up and drain away from a door, and a guard has to fix to whatever is left. Establishes the structural relationship first, since a cantilevered slab, a thermally broken connection and an independently supported balcony are three different buildings, then works the falls, the threshold, the drainage and the guard. Trigger on "balcony detail", "cantilever slab thermal bridge", "terrace over occupied space", "balcony door threshold", "where does the balcony drain", "design-balcony", or a projecting floor with a wall behind it. Carries no dimensions, no thermal values, no loads and no code requirements; guard and drainage requirements are code you quote and the structure is the engineer's. It does not replace the Architect of Record.

This is a skill. It runs in Chat, Cowork and Claude Code.
