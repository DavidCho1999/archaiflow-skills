# Notice Letter Template

**FOR COUNSEL REVIEW BEFORE SERVICE.** This template is procedural — it provides a standard form for a Notice of Claim under AIA A201-2017 §15.1.3 (or parallel sections in other contract forms). Counsel reviews and finalizes language and confirms delivery method before service.

Adjust per the actual contract form and dispute particulars.

## Procedural notes for the user (delete before service)

1. **Verify the delivery method** matches A201-2017 §1.5.2 — written, addressed to the parties at addresses in the Agreement, by hand / certified mail / recognized commercial courier.
2. **Verify the 21-day clock** — the clock runs from **recognition of condition** OR **event**, whichever is later. Recognition is typically when the affected party first knew or should have known of the condition giving rise to the Claim.
3. **Verify the IDM identity** — A201 §4.6 default is the Architect; the executed Agreement may name a different IDM. Send copy to the actual IDM.
4. **Verify the contract form** — this template tracks A201-2017. EJCDC C-700, ConsensusDOCS 200, and Federal CDA have different requirements (see `references/notice_timing_matrix.md`).
5. **Engage counsel before service** if any counsel-engagement trigger has fired (per `references/counsel_engagement_triggers.md`).
6. **Retain proof of delivery** — return receipt for certified mail, signed delivery confirmation for courier, signed acknowledgment for hand delivery.
7. **Calendar the next deadline** — Initial Decision (no specific deadline under §15.2.1 but typically reasonable time, often 30–60 days); if Initial Decision is rendered, demand for mediation must be filed within 30 days under §15.2.5.

This template is a starting form. **Counsel review and final approval before service is required when any of the counsel-engagement triggers in `references/counsel_engagement_triggers.md` apply.**
