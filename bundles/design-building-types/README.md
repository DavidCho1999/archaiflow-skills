# Design

Every Design tool in one plugin: 36 skills, 19 agents and 1 command for North American architecture practice.

## Skills

- **Adjacency Matrix**: Build the matrix from who actually moves, not from who says they are close
- **Area Measurement**: Establish which area standard governs and what actually counts in it
- **Brief Review**: Interrogate a brief for gaps, contradictions, and unstated assumptions
- **Budget Reconciliation**: Close the gap between the programme and the budget, and price what goes
- **Circulation Study**: Trace each journey through the plan and find where two that should not meet do
- **Daylight Study**: Work out how deep daylight reaches into each room, and which rooms stay dark
- **Design Addition**: Work out how new construction meets the building already there
- **Design Bathroom**: Lay out a bathroom around the plumbing wall and the door swing
- **Design Core**: Add up the core from your own dimensions and check its share of the plate
- **Design Critic**: Get a structured critique of a design
- **Design Entry**: Work the entry as a sequence from the street to the second door
- **Design Interior**: Decide what the interior has to do before detailing any part of it
- **Design Kitchen**: Lay out a kitchen around the work that happens in it, and the services
- **Design Millwork**: Detail built-in joinery so the shop can price it and the wall can hold it
- **Design Narrative**: Write what the project is, for a reader who was not in the room
- **Design Partition**: Build up an interior partition and decide where it stops
- **Elevation Review**: Read an elevation for what it decides and what it flattens away
- **Existing Conditions Survey**: Record what is actually there, and mark what you did not verify
- **Fit-Out Study**: Fit a programme into a shell somebody else built
- **Option Compare**: Build a side-by-side comparison of design options for a decision
- **Parti Study**: Find the one idea the scheme is actually organized by, and test it
- **Phasing Study**: Plan construction phases around a building that stays open through them
- **Plan Review**: Read a plan for what is on it, what is missing, and what it implies
- **Precedent Critique**: Critique your scheme through lenses drawn from precedents you supply
- **Precedent Finder**: Find architectural precedents for a concept or condition
- **Program Reader**: Read a brief or programme document for what its numbers rest on
- **Project Website Builder**: Turn a project PDF or images into one web page you can send as a link
- **Section Review**: Read a section for what it resolves and what it draws around
- **Security Design Review**: Read a plan for how it will be misused, not for what it is called
- **Shadow Study**: Compute sun angles and shadow reach for a massing, date, and place
- **Site Analysis**: Read a site for what it decides, before anything is drawn on it
- **Sketch Reader**: Read a sketch, an image, or a model view and say what it commits to
- **Space Program**: Track the room list, target areas, and designed areas as they move
- **Stacking Diagram**: Stack the programme against what the floor plate and the core will actually take
- **Test Fit**: Run the arithmetic on what fits a site under your limits
- **View Analysis**: Work out what each room sees, and what protects it

## Agents

- **Adaptive Reuse Architect**: A reuse architect on what the building allows, and what it costs to find out
- **Civic Architect**: A civic architect's read on a government building
- **Healthcare Architect**: A healthcare architect's read on a hospital or clinic plan
- **Higher Education Architect**: A campus architect's read on a university project
- **Historic Preservation Architect**: A preservation architect's read on work to a historic building
- **Hospitality Architect**: A hotel architect on the guest sequence, key count, and back of house
- **Industrial Architect**: An industrial architect on the clear span, the dock, and the yard
- **Interior Architect**: An interior architect's read on finishes, partitions, and millwork
- **K-12 Architect**: A K-12 architect's read on a school plan
- **Lab Architect**: A lab architect on the module, the services, and what the science needs
- **Library Architect**: A library architect on a building type that changed under everyone
- **Multifamily Architect**: A housing architect on the unit mix, the plate, and the core
- **Residential Architect**: A custom-home architect on the site, the plan, and the budget not yet said
- **Restaurant Architect**: A restaurant architect on the kitchen, the covers, and the queue
- **Retail Architect**: A retail architect on the frontage, the flow, and the landlord's rules
- **Senior Architect**: A 25-year architect's gut-check on what will break the project
- **Senior Living Architect**: A senior-living architect's read on an aging-care project
- **Sports & Recreation Architect**: A recreation architect on the wet side, the dry side, and the noise
- **Workplace Architect**: A workplace architect on density, the core, and how people actually work

## Commands

- **Test Massing**: Compare massing options by computed GFA, height, and net area

## Install

In the Claude app: Customize > Plugins > Add marketplace, paste `DavidCho1999/archaiflow-skills`, then install **design-building-types**.

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin install design-building-types --marketplace DavidCho1999/archaiflow-skills
```

Browse and read each one at https://archaiflow.com/templates/design-building-types.
