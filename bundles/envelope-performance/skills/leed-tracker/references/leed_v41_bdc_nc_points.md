# LEED v4.1 BD+C: New Construction — Credit & Point Matrix

**Source**: USGBC LEED v4.1 BD+C: NC scorecard (publicly available on usgbc.org/leed/v41).

All point values, credit names, and category caps below are quoted verbatim from the USGBC scorecard. Strategic guidance and credit *technical requirements* live in the LEED v4.1 BD+C Reference Guide (paywalled) and are NOT reproduced here.

> **Format note**: `P` = Prerequisite (mandatory, no points). `Max` = maximum points achievable for the credit. Multi-path credits earn anywhere from 1 to `Max` based on which path the project documents.

## Certification thresholds

| Level | Points |
|-------|--------|
| Certified | 40–49 |
| Silver | 50–59 |
| Gold | 60–79 |
| Platinum | 80–110 |

Note: LT's individual credits (Cr 2–8) sum to 16. LT Cr 1 (LEED ND Location) at max 16 is *alternative* — projects either earn it OR pursue the other LT credits, not both fully. The skill enforces the LT category cap at 16.
