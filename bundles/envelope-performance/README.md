# Envelope

Every Envelope tool in one plugin: 24 skills, 3 agents and 7 commands for North American architecture practice.

## Skills

- **Air Barrier Trace**: Follow the air, water, vapor, and thermal layers around a section for breaks
- **Airtightness Tracker**: Track one airtightness target from the spec through the site to the test
- **Assembly Review**: Read an assembly somebody else built up and say what it does not do
- **Condensation Check**: Trace the temperature gradient through an assembly and find the dew point
- **Curtain Wall Submittal Review**: Read a curtain wall submittal for the things a general review will not catch
- **Carbon Roadmap**: Plan a building's path to lower carbon over 5-15 years
- **Design Balcony**: Carry structure, water, and insulation past the wall without losing any
- **Design Below-Grade Assembly**: Build up a below-grade wall or slab where water comes first and drying does not
- **Design Envelope**: Decide what each face of the building has to be, before detailing any
- **Design Expansion Joint**: Place movement joints and carry the control layers across a gap that moves
- **Design Parapet**: Carry the wall and roof control layers around the one corner that leaks most
- **Design Roof Assembly**: Build up a roof assembly and place the control layers a roof cannot dry through
- **Design Roof Drainage**: Get water off the roof, and say where it goes when a drain blocks
- **Design Wall Assembly**: Build up an exterior wall layer by layer and place its four control layers
- **Design Window Perimeter**: Detail a window head, jamb, and sill in the order they get installed
- **Embodied Carbon Tracker**: Track embodied carbon by stage against factors you supply
- **Envelope Checklist**: Get the envelope checklist for the phase and the assemblies you have
- **Envelope Diff**: Compare two versions of an assembly or a detail and price the change
- **Facade Access Study**: Work out how every face of the envelope gets reached, cleaned, and replaced
- **Facade Module**: Divide an elevation into panels and lay out the leftover, option by option
- **Leak Triage**: Find out which water this is before anyone opens anything up
- **LEED Tracker**: Score a project scorecard and predict its certification level
- **Mockup Review**: Review a built mockup against what the documents actually asked for
- **Passive House Tracker**: Track modeled performance against certification thresholds

## Agents

- **Enclosure Architect**: An enclosure architect's read on a wall, roof, or window detail
- **Energy Modeler**: An energy modeler's read on a performance model
- **Senior Waterproofing Consultant**: A waterproofing consultant's read on anything that has to stay dry and buried

## Commands

- **Compare Assembly**: Put two or more envelope build-ups side by side on criteria you name
- **Explain U-value**: Check an assembly's R-value against your climate zone
- **Explain Vapor Retarder**: Explain what a vapor retarder does, and what it is being asked to do here
- **Lookup R-value**: Look up an assembly R-value or U-factor
- **Size Louver**: Add up the louver face a mechanical airflow needs, from data you supply
- **Test Energy**: Check one assembly against the energy table for your zone
- **Test Glazing**: Check a window against the energy table for your zone

