## Disclaimers

This memo is a recommendation from the owner's-side review perspective. It is **not**:

- **Broker advice** — no premium quoted, no policy bound. The licensed broker delivers the actual market analysis and binds policies.
- **Insurance counsel** — no advice on contract enforcement, claims handling, or coverage disputes.
- **State-specific compliance certification** — state Workers' Comp, Auto, and contractor licensing rules vary; broker / counsel verifies.
- **Bond adequacy opinion** — surety market governs; refer to broker / surety agent.

The recommended limits and structure are heuristic starting points (per `policy_types_reference.md`); the broker's market analysis governs the actual program.
