# Discipline Routing Table

Map a site question to the responsible consultant. When a question crosses disciplines, the *primary* discipline goes in the To: field; CC the secondary.

## Architecture (To: Architect)

Topics:
- Partition layouts, room dimensions
- Door/window types, hardware schedules
- Finishes (flooring, ceiling, paint, tile)
- Reflected ceiling plan
- Casework, millwork
- Code/egress questions on plans
- Roof system (envelope)
- Wet-wall waterproofing

## Structural (To: Structural Engineer)

Topics:
- Beam/column sizes, framing layout
- Foundation, slab thickness, rebar
- Steel connections, anchor bolts
- Load-bearing wall changes
- Cantilever, deflection limits
- Penetration sleeves through structural members

## MEP — Mechanical (To: Mechanical Engineer)

Topics:
- HVAC duct sizing, routing
- Equipment schedules (AHU, VRF, FCU)
- Diffusers, return locations
- Thermal zoning
- Refrigerant piping

## MEP — Electrical (To: Electrical Engineer)

Topics:
- Panel schedules, circuits, load calcs
- Lighting fixture types and switching
- Receptacle layouts
- Low-voltage / data
- Fire alarm devices
- Emergency power

## MEP — Plumbing (To: Plumbing Engineer)

Topics:
- Fixture schedules
- Hot/cold water routing, slope
- Sanitary, vent stacks
- Floor drains, roof drains
- Domestic water heater

## Civil (To: Civil Engineer)

Topics:
- Site grading, drainage
- Utility tie-ins (sewer, water main)
- Paving, curb cuts
- Site retaining walls

## Landscape (To: Landscape Architect)

Topics:
- Planting, irrigation
- Hardscape patterns
- Site furniture
- Site lighting (often shared with Electrical)

## Interior (To: Interior Designer)

Topics:
- FF&E
- Decorative finishes (where ID holds spec, not Architecture)
- Drapery, signage graphics, decorative lighting

## Cross-discipline conventions

- **Ducts/conduit penetrating structure**: Primary = MEP requesting, CC Structural.
- **Roof system disagreements**: Primary = Architecture, CC Structural for connection details.
- **Wet wall waterproofing**: Primary = Architecture, CC Plumbing for fixture coordination.
- **Mechanical room layout**: Primary = MEP, CC Architecture for clearances.
- **Curtain wall**: Primary = Architecture, CC Structural for embedded plates and Glazing subcontractor.
- **Site lighting**: Primary = Landscape if pole/fixture aesthetic; Primary = Electrical if circuiting/control.
