# North American Program Benchmarks

Order-of-magnitude benchmarks for sanity-checking schedule and budget assumptions in a brief. Sources: RSMeans, GSA P-100, BOMA Experience Exchange Report, DOE FRPP, NIH Design Policy and Guidelines, and AIA Compensation Survey. Update when newer editions are published.

These are **planning-grade** numbers, not estimates. Use them to flag when a brief's budget/area is implausible, then defer to a real cost consultant for actual estimates.

---

## Cost per gross square foot (US, Q1 2026, mid-range US metro)

Construction cost only — excludes A/E fees, FF&E, owner soft costs, and land. Adjust ±25% for high-cost coastal markets (NYC, SF, LA, Boston, Seattle, DC) or low-cost interior markets.

| Building type | $/gsf range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tenant fit-out (Class A office) | 90 – 180 | Excludes shell |

| Office shell & core (Type IIB low-rise) | 280 – 400 | |

| Higher-ed classroom / lab | 550 – 900 | Wet lab pushes high end |

## Soft cost percentages (of construction cost)

| Item | Typical % | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A/E basic services | 6 – 12% | Smaller projects higher |
| A/E additional services | 1 – 4% | LEED, BIM coordination, etc. |
| Owner's rep / PM | 1 – 3% | |

| Permits + impact fees | 1 – 5% | Highly jurisdiction-dependent (CA, NYC > 5%) |
| FF&E | 5 – 15% | Hospitality, healthcare on the high end |
| AV / IT / security | 2 – 8% | Lab, healthcare on the high end |
| Legal + insurance | 0.5 – 1.5% | |
| Owner contingency | 5 – 10% | |
| **Total soft costs** | **18 – 28%** | Typical NA range |

## Gross-to-net ratios

Rough multipliers from net assignable area (nsf) to gross floor area (gsf). High end means more efficient program (single-tenant office), low end means more support (lab, healthcare).

| Building type | Gross-to-net |
|---|---|
| Office (single tenant) | 1.15 – 1.20 |
| Office (multi-tenant) | 1.20 – 1.30 |
| K-12 school | 1.50 – 1.70 |
| Higher-ed classroom | 1.40 – 1.60 |
| Higher-ed lab | 1.55 – 1.80 |
| Multifamily | 1.20 – 1.35 |
| Hotel | 1.30 – 1.50 |
| Hospital | 1.60 – 1.90 |
| MOB | 1.40 – 1.55 |
| Lab (wet) | 1.55 – 1.85 |

## Schedule benchmarks (typical NA, full-service procurement)

For sanity-checking briefs that promise unrealistic timelines.

| Phase | Mid-size project (50-150k gsf) |
|---|---|
| Programming + brief | 4 – 12 weeks |
| Schematic Design (SD) | 6 – 12 weeks |
| Design Development (DD) | 8 – 16 weeks |
| Construction Documents (CD) | 12 – 24 weeks |
| Bidding / negotiation | 4 – 8 weeks |
| Permit review (typical) | 6 – 16 weeks |
| Permit review (complex AHJ — NYC DOB, CA OSHPD/HCAI, DSA) | 6 – 18 months |
| Construction (50-150k gsf typical) | 12 – 24 months |
| Commissioning + closeout | 4 – 12 weeks |

## When a benchmark does not apply

If the project type is not covered (e.g., aquariums, prisons, data centers, semiconductor fabs, missions), say so in the review and recommend the owner engage a cost consultant with sector-specific experience before finalizing the brief. Do not extrapolate from adjacent typologies for these.
